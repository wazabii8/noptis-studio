NOPTIS STUDIO

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy explains how NOPTIS Studio handles information when you use the web application or its installed version, including the version distributed through the Microsoft Store.

1. Local data

NOPTIS Studio is designed to work locally by default. You can use its core features without creating an application account. Saved NOPTIS identifiers (GIDs), descriptions, lists, SQL queries and application preferences are stored on your device. This information is used to provide the application's features and may contain personal information if you choose to include it.

2. Optional remote synchronization

You can connect a remoteStorage-compatible account to synchronize your saved data across devices. Synchronization is optional and is initiated by connecting your chosen storage account and authorizing access.

When connected, the application processes your storage account identifier (which may resemble an email address), connection and authorization information, and synchronization status. Saved identifiers, descriptions, lists and SQL queries are transferred between your device and the storage provider you select to provide synchronization.

Your storage provider operates independently and handles your account and synchronized information according to its own privacy policy and terms. NOPTIS Studio does not operate a central storage service for your synchronized content.

One compatible provider is 5apps Storage, operated by AppCache Ltd. If you choose 5apps, its Privacy Policy explains how it handles account information, service usage, data storage, international transfers and data deletion. If you choose another provider, consult that provider's own privacy policy and terms. NOPTIS Studio is not a service operated by 5apps.

3. Hosting and distribution

The application is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for information about GitHub's processing.

If you install the application through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may process information in connection with Store distribution and platform services. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

4. Usage analytics

NOPTIS Studio uses GoatCounter to count visits to the application without setting analytics cookies. The counting script can send the page path and title, the referring page, URL query parameters, and screen width. Depending on the analytics settings, visit statistics may include browser, operating system, language, and approximate location. Saved GIDs, descriptions, and SQL queries are excluded from analytics events.

GoatCounter can use the IP address to estimate location. If session counting is enabled, GoatCounter keeps the IP address and the browser’s User-Agent header in memory for up to eight hours to recognize repeat visits. Raw IP addresses and full User-Agent headers stay out of its analytics database. See the GoatCounter Privacy Policy for details.

5. Storage, security and retention

Local information is retained on your device until you remove it or clear the application's stored data. Remote information is retained according to your storage provider's settings and policies and until you delete it, subject to the provider's retention practices.

The application is served over HTTPS. For remote synchronization, use a trusted provider with HTTPS support. Access to remote data is controlled through the authorization granted to the application and your provider's security controls. Local data is subject to the security of your device and browser or installed web application environment. Do not include passwords, access tokens or other sensitive information in saved queries or descriptions.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Use the application without connecting a remote storage account.
  • View, edit or delete your saved identifiers, lists and queries in the application.
  • Disconnect remote storage from the Synchronization page to stop synchronization. You can also revoke access through your provider where supported.
  • Clear local application data using your browser or operating system's application storage controls. This may permanently remove unsynchronized data.
  • Manage or delete remote copies through your storage provider. Disconnecting does not necessarily delete previously synchronized data.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal information, and to request portability. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. In Sweden, this is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application or its data handling changes. The latest version will be available on this page, with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning NOPTIS Studio, contact the developer through the developer's GitHub profile. Please do not post personal or confidential information in public GitHub issues.
