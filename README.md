# NOPTIS Studio

![NOPTIS Studio - Inspect NOPTIS GIDs instantly](https://wazabii.se/github-assets/noptis-studio/hero.png)

**Turn NOPTIS GIDs into something you can actually read.**

[Open NOPTIS Studio](https://wazabii8.github.io/noptis-studio/)

A NOPTIS GID may look like a long string of numbers, but every part of it has a meaning.

NOPTIS Studio reveals that structure instantly. Paste a GID and get a clear breakdown of its type, segments, and values, without manually counting characters or searching through documentation.

Whether you are troubleshooting a vehicle, checking a journey, validating a stop, or simply trying to understand an unfamiliar identifier, NOPTIS Studio gives you the answer at a glance.

## What it does

NOPTIS Studio provides live inspection of NOPTIS GIDs directly in the browser.

- Detects the GID type from its prefix
- Breaks the identifier into named segments
- Displays the meaning and value of each segment
- Validates the expected structure and segment lengths
- Supports English and Swedish
- Lets you add familiar names to identifiers such as TPA and TPO numbers
- Keeps those names locally on your device

There is no account to create and no data needs to be uploaded to a server. Enter a GID, inspect it, and continue with your work.

## Use it as an app

NOPTIS Studio is a Progressive Web App. You can install it from the browser and open it from your desktop, Start menu, Dock, taskbar, or phone home screen without first navigating to the website.

Installing it is optional, but it makes the tool much quicker to reach when you regularly work with NOPTIS identifiers.

### Chrome on Windows, macOS, or Linux

1. Open [NOPTIS Studio](https://wazabii8.github.io/noptis-studio/) in Chrome.
2. Click the install icon in the address bar, when shown.
3. Choose **Install**.

When the icon is not visible, open the Chrome menu and look for **Install NOPTIS Studio** or **Cast, save and share → Install page as app**. The exact wording can vary between Chrome versions.

### Microsoft Edge on Windows or macOS

1. Open [NOPTIS Studio](https://wazabii8.github.io/noptis-studio/) in Edge.
2. Open the **Settings and more** menu (`…`).
3. Select **Apps → Install NOPTIS Studio**.
4. Choose where Edge should place shortcuts, such as the taskbar, Start menu, or desktop.

### Safari on macOS

On recent versions of macOS:

1. Open [NOPTIS Studio](https://wazabii8.github.io/noptis-studio/) in Safari.
2. Choose **File → Add to Dock**.
3. Confirm the name and select **Add**.

NOPTIS Studio will then appear in the Dock and Applications like a regular app.

### iPhone or iPad

1. Open [NOPTIS Studio](https://wazabii8.github.io/noptis-studio/) in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Select **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

The app will open from its own icon without the normal Safari interface.

### Android

1. Open [NOPTIS Studio](https://wazabii8.github.io/noptis-studio/) in Chrome.
2. Open the browser menu (`⋮`).
3. Select **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
4. Confirm the installation.

The available wording depends on the browser and Android version.

## Found something wrong?

NOPTIS Studio is open source, and reports from people who work with real identifiers are valuable. A GID layout may be incorrect, a label may be unclear, a translation may need adjustment, or an identifier may behave differently from what the application expects.

Please [create an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/wazabii8/noptis-studio/issues/new) and include:

- The GID or GID type involved, when it is safe to share
- What NOPTIS Studio displayed
- What you expected it to display
- Your browser and device, when the problem appears platform-specific

Feature suggestions are also welcome through the [issue tracker](https://github.com/wazabii8/noptis-studio/issues).

## Privacy

The GIDs you inspect and the custom names you save remain in your browser. NOPTIS Studio does not require an account and is intended to be a simple tool you can open and use immediately.
